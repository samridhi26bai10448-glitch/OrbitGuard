Space Sustainability

Understanding orbital pollution and protecting Earth's orbital environment.

SPACE ENVIRONMENT
🌍

Protect Earth's Orbit

Earth's orbital environment supports communication, navigation, weather forecasting and scientific missions. Keeping these orbital regions usable is essential for future generations.

🛰️

Reduce Orbital Debris

Defunct satellites, spent rocket stages and fragments can remain in orbit for years. Responsible mission planning and debris mitigation help limit their growth.

⚠️

Understand Collision Risks

Objects in crowded orbital regions require monitoring. Reliable conjunction assessment helps satellite operators evaluate potential close approaches.

♻️

Responsible Space Operations

End-of-life disposal, passivation and responsible spacecraft design can reduce the creation of additional orbital debris.

OrbitGuard's mission

Support informed orbital awareness through satellite tracking, debris monitoring and transparent space-environment analytics.
